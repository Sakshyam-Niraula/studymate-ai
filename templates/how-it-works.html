How StudyMate AI works

StudyMate AI is designed around a simple study workflow: provide your material, understand it, and practice it.

STEP 01

Upload your study material

Start by uploading a PDF containing your notes or other study material.

STEP 02

Ask questions

Ask questions about your uploaded material and StudyMate will help explain the relevant concepts.

STEP 03

Generate a quiz

Create multiple-choice questions based on your study material and choose a difficulty level.

STEP 04

Check your understanding

Complete the quiz, see your score, and review explanations for the questions.
