About StudyMate AI

StudyMate AI is an AI-powered study assistant designed to help students learn from their own study material.

The idea

Studying often means going through long notes, searching for important concepts, and creating practice questions manually. StudyMate AI brings those activities into one simple study workflow.

What StudyMate can do

Students can upload PDF study material, ask questions about it, and generate multiple-choice quizzes for practice.

Built for students

The goal is to make AI-assisted studying simple, accessible, and focused on the material a student actually needs to learn.
